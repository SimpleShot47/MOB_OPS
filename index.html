2. Mission Timeline & Operating Flow

Mission Planning 00:00-00:30

CAOC, MOBs, and CSpOC conduct joint planning, order reviews, political assessments, RFIs, and aircraft apportionment.

Mission Pre-Brief 00:30-00:45

Mission Commanders brief teams and instructors. Missed RFIs are answered before execution.

Turn 1 Execution 00:45-01:10

MOBs report final ATOs, fighter munitions, and risk tokens to CAOC for execution.

Turn 2 Planning 01:10-01:35

Plan the subsequent turn based on updated threats, RFIs, aircraft availability, and CSpOC effects.

Turn 2 Execution 01:35-02:00

Execute second-turn ATOs, risk tokens, munitions, and final MOB-to-CAOC reporting.

Hotwash / Debrief 02:00-02:20

Instructor feedback, mission effects, decisions, gaps, and lessons learned.

8. High-Priority Watch Items

  • Satellite jammer within 2 hexes: request SDA confirmation and Orbital Warfare option.
  • Airborne jammer near strike route: recommend cyber/GPS support or delay sortie.
  • Enemy fighters near mobility route: recommend escort, reroute, or alternate FOS.
  • COMM/Data denial: shift to verbal backup and request COMM satellite coverage.
  • Missile threat: advise dispersion, missile defense, hardening, or FOS transition.

12. MOB LNO Operating Reminders

  • Your value is speed. Report early enough to shape ATO, FOS, and strike decisions.
  • Use Threat-Impact-Recommendation. Do not just pass raw CSpOC data.
  • Think one phase ahead. Satellite movement and denied communications matter before execution.
  • Protect the decision cycle. The MOB commander needs concise, relevant, actionable updates.
  • Execution windows matter most. Be ready before Turn 1 Execution and Turn 2 Execution.

13. Quick Verbal Phrases

“MOB, CSpOC update: [threat] located at [location]. Impact is [impact]. Recommend [action].” “CSpOC, MOB request: need [space effect] over [location] by [phase/turn/time] to support [mission].” “Commander, this affects [FOS/fighter/mobility/COMM]. My recommendation is [action] because [reason].”
